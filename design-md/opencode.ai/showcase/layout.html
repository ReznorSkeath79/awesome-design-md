FAQ

+ Does Northwind store my data?

No. It reads your warehouse in place and keeps only the query and its result cache. The cloud tier is opt-in per region.

− Which warehouses are supported?

Postgres, MySQL, BigQuery, Snowflake, Redshift, S3, and any source you can expose as a CSV drop or webhook.

+ Can I run it without internet?

Yes. The installer bundles the local runtime; models download once and run offline after that.

+ How is pricing measured?

Per connected region, per month. The Free tier covers one region and five dashboards; Pro and Max scale from there.
