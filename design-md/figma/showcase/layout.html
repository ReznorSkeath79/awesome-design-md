How it works

From raw feeds to a decision in four steps.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Point Northwind at your feeds — spreadsheets, warehouse tables, or an API.

  2. 02

    Model

    Map fields to the shared dictionary once; the rollups maintain themselves.

  3. 03

    Monitor

    Dashboards and alerts update live as movements land, region by region.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Export a decision-ready view, or share a governed link with the region.

Ship

Publish the model, not another workbook.

Give every region a governed view it can trust. Northwind keeps the definitions, versions, and lineage behind the numbers — so shipping an update is a decision, not a merge.
