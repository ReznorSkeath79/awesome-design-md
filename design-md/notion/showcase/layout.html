Enterprise

Security and scale, without the sprawl.

How does Northwind keep totals honest?

Metrics are defined once and recomputed everywhere from the ledger, so a board, a dashboard, and an export always agree. Every value traces back to its source row.

Can we scope access by region?

Yes. Row-level access is scoped by region and role, and each grant or change is written to the audit log for review.

What about SSO and provisioning?

SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning are included on Business and Enterprise, along with enforced two-factor for every member.
