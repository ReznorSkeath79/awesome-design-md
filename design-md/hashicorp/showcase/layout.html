The Northwind family

Three products, one operations stack

Each surface owns a different job — region control, data throughput, and live metrics — and carries its own identity color through the platform.

Northwind Atlas

Region control plane

Model every site, gateway, and replica as one graph. Atlas keeps a single declarative picture of your footprint and reconciles drift on its own.

  • Declarative region graph
  • Drift detection every 60s
  • Policy as versioned code
Explore Atlas
Northwind Transit

Data throughput fabric

Move events between regions without losing a row. Transit buffers, batches, and replays regional streams into one ordered feed.

  • Exactly-once regional handoff
  • Replay from any watermark
  • Backpressure you can see
Explore Transit
Northwind Pulse

Live regional metrics

Roll throughput, latency, and cost into dashboards your operators trust — sliced by region, product line, or shift.

  • Sub-2s streaming panels
  • Anomaly alerts per region
  • Shareable ops scorecards
Explore Pulse
Northwind Atlas

Region control, end to end

See every region as it actually runs

Atlas reads your live footprint instead of a spreadsheet. Change one region, preview the blast radius, and promote it everywhere on your own schedule.
