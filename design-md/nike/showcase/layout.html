What is Northwind?

Northwind is an operations analytics platform. It turns the raw ledger of a busy floor — shipments, lanes, shifts, revenue — into dashboards and forecasts your team can act on the same hour.

Where does my data live?

In the region you choose at sign-up. Six regions are available today, each with read-local latency and automatic failover, and every access is written to an audit log.

How fast can we start?

Import from your current tracker and the first dashboard is usually live the same day. No migration project, no data-warehouse rebuild required.
