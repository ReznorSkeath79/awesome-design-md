How it works

From raw signal to a decision in three steps

  1. 01Connect a source. Historians, spreadsheets, and APIs all land in the same model.
  2. 02Define a metric once. Northwind reconciles every definition across teams.
  3. 03Share the board. Everyone reads the same number at the same moment.
