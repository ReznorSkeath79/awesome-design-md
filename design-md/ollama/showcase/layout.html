Frequently asked questions

Where does Northwind store my data?

On your own machines and in the warehouse you connect. Nothing is copied to us by default — the cloud tier is opt-in per region.

Do I need a GPU to run it?

A laptop with 16 GB of memory runs the Free tier comfortably. Heavier models in the Max tier want a workstation or a data-center node.

Can I bring my own metrics?

Yes. Point Northwind at a warehouse, a CSV drop, or a webhook and it will model the table on the next sync.

Is there a free trial of Max?

Every plan starts with 14 days of Max, no card required. Downgrade or cancel any time from the account page.
