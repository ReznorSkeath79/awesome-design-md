07

FAQ

Questions answered.

The five questions leadership asked most often while this brief was being drafted.

What exactly do staff get?

An assistant inside the tools they already use: answers from the site knowledge base, draft replies, ticket summaries and routing suggestions. No new login beyond single sign-on, and nothing changes for customers.

How are the three sites chosen?

One high-volume site, one mid-volume site and one site with a newer knowledge base, so the pilot tests the assistant across real spread rather than three lookalikes.

What happens at week twelve?

The programme owner brings the scorecard and a recommendation to leadership: scale, extend the pilot, or stop. The decision is recorded and owned by leadership, not the pilot team.

How is success measured?

Three numbers against the two-week baseline: adoption among eligible staff, hours saved per week, and answer-quality checks. All three appear on the weekly scorecard.

What does it cost if we scale?

Setup is largely one-off, so per-site cost falls beyond the pilot: the estimate holds site cost flat and only the run cost grows. A scale figure is part of the week-twelve pack, not this brief.
