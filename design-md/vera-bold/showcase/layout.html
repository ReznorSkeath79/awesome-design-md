Three ways in

PICK A LANE.

The pilot can be run managed, assisted or automatic. We recommend the managed lane: the assistant drafts, a person always decides.

A
Managed pilot12 weeks · 3 sites
Recommended

Northwind Assist drafts; a named reviewer clears every answer. Measured against the 2026 baseline before any scale decision.

B
Assisted rollout8 weeks · 2 sites
Viable

Faster and lighter, but only two sites and a thinner baseline — harder to defend a scale-up on the evidence alone.

C
Automatic sendNo pilot · live
Not advised

Answers go straight to customers with no reviewer. We have neither the guardrails nor the evidence to accept that risk today.
