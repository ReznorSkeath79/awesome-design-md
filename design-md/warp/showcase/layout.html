Dashboards that behave

Build a board once and it stays legible as data grows — no reflow, no clipping, no last-minute fixes before the review.

An agent that reads

Ask in plain language; the agent queries the warehouse, cites the rows, and drafts the summary you were going to write.

Governed by default

Row-level policy and per-team quotas, evaluated at query time, so every region sees exactly its own numbers.

Your workflow, already in the terminal.

Northwind ships as an environment, not a portal. Pipe rollups, schedule agents, and keep your shell prompt exactly where it belongs.
