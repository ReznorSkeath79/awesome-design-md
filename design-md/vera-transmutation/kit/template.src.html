01
Thesis

Say it once, plainly

State the plan in one sentence a leader can repeat, then let the rest of the page earn it.

The one sentence

Write the thesis before any visual. If a leader cannot repeat it after one read, the page is not ready. Keep it concrete: what changes, for whom, and by when.

What follows
  • Twelve evidence items carry the argument.
  • Four decision asks close the page.
  • Every figure below already appears in the text.
◐
Rule of the page
The thesis frames the debate; the evidence settles it. Do not open with history — open with the claim.

07
Risks

What can stop this

Name each risk and the mitigation beside it; never leave a risk without an owner action.

RiskMitigation
Privacy and consentSpoken and on-screen consent before any recording; a stated retention window.
Bias and adverse impactRubric-anchored scoring with verbatim evidence; four-fifths monitoring; human override.
AccuracyPhrase lists, then a tuned model; a measured error threshold as the gate.
Prompt injectionThe engine owns the flow; inputs are data, never instructions.
CheatingFocus and paste signals go to human review, never an automatic fail.
⚠
Show every flag to a human
A flag is a prompt to review, not a verdict. Keep the reader, not the model, in control of the outcome.
